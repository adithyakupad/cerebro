# Negative findings and alternative explanations

Historical protocols and outcomes are preserved. This integration changes no success threshold, split or scientific result.

| Question | Outcome | What this rules out | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| M022 fixed mitigation priority | All seven candidates fail the frozen gate | Promotion of any tested candidate under this protocol | Universal impossibility of useful adaptation |
| Calibration allocation | Balanced15 lowers MSE on all three dates but uses extra commands/later trials | A fair claim of free, chronology-matched improvement | Physiological drift or causal command-coverage mechanism |
| Finite calibration | Empirical prediction dispersion decreases with budget | Ignoring calibration-subset variability | Physiological bias or independent biological replication |
| Penalty and scaling | Effects vary by date | A universal remedy from the tested settings | Optimal regularization across future recordings |
| M023 reliability | All eligible candidates fail; combined worse than label-only | Neural-specific predictive reliability from these fixed features/models | Failure of every possible reliability estimator |
| M023 temporal primary | Below 5% versus mean/permuted controls | Promotion based on persistence gains alone | Absence of observed temporal structure |
| Dynamics risk extension | Primary risk error worsens 1.11% beyond task/label moments | Added benefit in this fixed comparison | Absence of all behaviorally relevant latent information |
| Physiology | Not identifiable from observation/task controls | Claims of diagnosed neural drift or causal connectivity | That physiology is unchanged |
| Later-session evaluation | Eligible, unscored by null rule | Claim of independent adaptation validation | A current data-access blocker |

One animal, three inspected development dates, overlapping calibration subsets, correlated bins, task mixture, contact rather than neuron identity, native unit-label inconsistency and absent quality masks remain limitations. Nonchronological session folds are not forward deployment; conditional fixed-fit trial-block intervals are not biological population intervals. FA likelihood warnings and their numerical cross-check remain disclosed.
