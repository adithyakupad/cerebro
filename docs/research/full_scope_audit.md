## Interpretation

Working software: real NWB acquisition, explicit coverage/clock validation, PCA/FA, classical and six modern decoder families, longitudinal calibration, SSL objectives and transfer controls, statistics, checkpoint/provenance registry, offline explorer and reports. Mission014 records a fresh Conda run of 46 commands and 222 local tests; current public CI executes 210 software tests and only collects 12 data cases. See `docs/development/mission_014_validation.md` and `.github/workflows/tests.yml`. These checks establish software behavior, not a general neural foundation model.

Scientific outcomes: LINK Feb05 frozen source MSE 0.03340539 exceeds source mean 0.02606807 flexion²; direct 75-label ridge reaches 0.02482701 against a matched mean of 0.02632579. New-date SSL differences are tiny and mixed. FALCON XDec10 direct 75-label ridge reaches 0.07758839 against target calibration mean 0.12855570 normalized EMG², but source-plus-residual is slightly worse than direct ridge. These are specific observations, not general transfer conclusions; see `reports/mission_014.md`.

External blockers: inspected FALCON targets are irreversibly author-preprocessed, with original EMG unavailable from inspected public assets; Perich lacks documented neural coverage. Two FALCON animals and one LINK animal limit independent population generalization. Obtaining data or approving scientific claims belongs to the human researcher.

Original-scope boundary: no original model family needs rebuilding. Speech is conditional on legal and scientific suitability. Low-latency streaming and actual biological closed-loop work are adjacent Neuralink-relevant competencies, not a hidden requirement for the existing offline research platform. Human publication approval is still required, but approval alone cannot supply missing data-validity evidence.
