## Questions, estimands and ownership

**A:** Can calibration-only information predict direct-ridge adaptation harm on later development trials? Outcome is direct alpha1000 equal-trial MSE minus frozen-source equal-trial MSE, in normalized flexion²; positive means harm. Predictions of that continuous contrast are primary; harm classification and a switch-to-adaptation policy are secondary. H1 command coverage, H2 finite-calibration variability, H3 neural shift and H4 decoder disagreement compete with a null of no useful predictive reliability.

**B:** Do two-stage linear latent dynamics forecast recorded neural counts beyond statistical controls, and add calibration-risk information beyond task coverage? This models observed activity, not identified physiological computation. Primary endpoint is one-step source-trained 6-dimensional PCA/AR forecast MSE in **source-standardized neural count²**, equally weighted across complete trials. Latent MSE is secondary and coordinate-dependent. Neither fitted covariance nor transition coefficients identify causal connectivity. This is static PCA/FA plus latent ridge AR, not a claim of jointly estimated maximum-likelihood LDS or Kalman smoothing.

The researcher retains model/estimand/interpretation ownership. We use prespecified existing classical methods, a parsimonious linear reliability model, 5% practical threshold and conservative null gates. These are reviewable methodological decisions, not internship or hiring qualification claims. Any owner change before outcomes is recorded prospectively; after outcomes no threshold, candidate or cohort expansion is allowed.

## A: calibration-only features and information budgets

Reuse all369 M022 recorded allocations and outcomes, but **recompute inputs** from calibration data; the primary reliability training/evaluation uses only20 uniform subsets/date/budget. Chronological and balanced allocations are secondary distribution-shift checks with no refitting. Outcome labels may use the allowed30 development evaluation trials, but reliability features may not access their neural samples, position samples, moments, command distribution or errors.

| Feature | Fixed calculation | Additional access |
|---|---|---|
| H1 coverage | Unique commanded conditions in B selected trials divided by number of source-training commands | B selected command labels, not all45 commands |
| H2 instability | Mean pointwise prediction variance across20 whole-trial resamples of the **same B** labeled trials, measured on pool45 neural observations | B position labels and45 unlabeled neural trials; no union of extra labeled subsets |
| H3 shift | Mean squared source-standardized selected-calibration neural mean | B neural trials, source scaler |
| H4 disagreement | Mean squared frozen/direct prediction difference on pool45 neural observations | B position labels and45 unlabeled neural trials |
| Label-only control | Selected-calibration position mean and variance for each coordinate, compared with source moments | B position labels |
| B dynamics mismatch | Source6d PCA/AR one-step reconstructed-neural prediction MSE on B calibration trials, divided by source-training forecast MSE | B neural trials, source model only |

All neural rows used as probes come from pool0:45; probes require no position labels. Direct models use original source scaler and selected alpha. Resample complete trials with replacement, fixed seeds23023 + stable allocation index; duplicate a trial only for fitting bootstrap diagnostics, never as a new independent biological observation. M022 `label_mean_distance_squared_posthoc` is **forbidden** as a predictor because it uses evaluation labels. M022 evaluation-ensemble prediction dispersion is **forbidden** as a B-label feature. Coverage accounting explicitly differs from original uniform-policy command-free selection.

Per budget, leave one entire development session out. Fit every reliability scaler and ridge parameter only on uniform subsets from the other two sessions; each session contributes equally. Ridge alpha1 is fixed, no hyperparameter search. Fit separate fixed models: coverage-only, instability-only, shift-only, disagreement-only, four-feature combined, label-only, coverage+label-only task control, and coverage+label-only+dynamics mismatch. Constant predicts training-session mean outcome; majority predicts training-session majority harm; a fixed seeded within-training-session outcome permutation supplies a negative control for the combined model. Fixed labels are shared across methods. No random row split across dates.

Primary risk prediction MSE is averaged over20 held-out allocations/date then equally over dates at B15. Harm balanced accuracy averages the two class recalls using pooled session-equally weighted allocations when both harm classes exist globally. Single-class fold balanced accuracy is undefined and reported as null. If globally only one class exists, balanced accuracy is undefined and the promotion gate fails; ordinary accuracy is never substituted. Also report class proportions, B5/B30 sensitivity, prediction stability under either training-date-only fit, and policy MSE when adapting only if predicted harm<0. A model predicting harm is not automatically an effective adaptation policy.

**Promotion priority:** disagreement-only, coverage-only, instability-only, combined. First passing model is chosen, never the best outcome. Require at least5% equal-date risk-MSE improvement versus constant, label-only and shift-only controls, strictly lower risk MSE than each on all three dates, conditional95% paired block interval below zero against each, and pooled session-weighted two-class balanced accuracy≥0.60 at B15. The shift-only model is the H3/control comparison, not promoted against itself. No choice among allocation policies or budgets. The dynamics extension is explanatory secondary evidence, not another unregistered promotion candidate.

For conditional uncertainty, resample the **same ordered evaluation-trial block indices across all overlapping calibration allocations within a session**, block5,1000 draws, seed23023, while holding fitted reliability parameters fixed. Recompute adaptation-difference labels from those trial losses. Average dates equally. These intervals cover conditional evaluation-trial variation, not training-label uncertainty, biological population uncertainty or independent subset replication. Report per-date results and differences between the two training-date-only risk fits rather than narrow claims from three session folds. No p-values or general population significance claim.

## B: controlled observed population dynamics

Reuse `fit_population`, `reconstruction`, `fit_decoder`, within-trial transitions and covariance/subspace helpers. PCA/FA dimensions3/6/12, ridge AR1 penalty1, behavior probe ridge1000, fixed seed23023. Primary source models fit only Jan27first300. Secondary local models fit first15 or45 pool trials; source scaling is primary, a local train-only scaler is a dimension6 sensitivity. No evaluation fit or model selection. Compute separately per trial, not across gaps.

Fit affine z[t+1]=A z[t]+b on training inferred latents. From actual x[t], propagate without teacher forcing for horizons1 and5, then reconstruct neural predictions. Compare identical valid time targets against persistence, training mean, a fixed within-training-trial time-permutation AR control, independent-channel AR and commanded-condition mean learned only from training trials. Condition control receives evaluation command metadata and is labeled accordingly; unknown commands fall back to training mean and are counted. Static held-out reconstruction is contemporaneous, not a forecast. FA Gaussian observation likelihood is descriptive model adequacy if finite, not a spike-count likelihood. Record both source-standardized neural error and model-coordinate latent error, actual elapsed native-clock distributions and spectral radius. No extrapolation across trial boundaries.

Offline behavior probes fit on each model's training latents/labels only and evaluate30 development trials; comparisons do not establish prospective BCI control. Geometry uses matched contact coordinates, training-set covariance and principal angles between equal-dimensional models; compare source/local and phase/condition-matched trajectories with minimum2 source and pool repetitions. Ten within-trial phase segments are **acausal descriptive normalization**, never forecasting inputs or physical movement-onset alignment. Store unmatched conditions/repetition counts. Orthogonal latent rotation must preserve reconstruction/forecasts and subspace angles to numerical tolerance. No visual manifold stability inference about identical neurons.

Primary temporal promotion is fixed source6d PCA/AR horizon1, never selected dimension/family/scaler: require≥5% mean reduction in neural forecast MSE versus persistence, training-mean and time-permuted controls, lower MSE on every development date, and paired conditional block interval below zero for each. FA, horizon5, dimensions and local fits are sensitivities, not fallback candidates. If this fixed model fails the gate, no untouched temporal outcome is consumed. Development still completes with negative/inconclusive evidence.
