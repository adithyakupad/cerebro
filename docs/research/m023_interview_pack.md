# CEREBRO technical interview evidence pack

Role pages checked **2026-10-18**: [Neuroengineer Intern, Next Gen team](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/neuralink/jobs/7483748003) and [Machine Learning Engineer Intern](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/neuralink/jobs/6594261003). These are preparation targets, not an assessment of hiring eligibility.

The neuroengineering posting emphasizes experimental judgment, interpreting neural and behavioral observations, population dynamics, decoding, and communicating results. CEREBRO offers inspectable **offline computational** evidence in those areas. It does not demonstrate experimental preparation, wet-lab work, electrophysiology acquisition, human fMRI, implant development, or online biological closed-loop control.

The machine-learning posting emphasizes decoder reliability, model fundamentals, analysis of complex data, clear communication, and maintainable implementation. CEREBRO offers code, frozen protocols, controls, tests, and negative findings that a researcher can defend. It does not establish clinical utility, production BCI reliability, or that its owner meets experience, education, or other hiring requirements. No applicant experience is inferred from repository contents.

## Evidence to bring

Bring the [M022 protocol](m022_protocol.md), [M022 report](../../reports/m022_calibration_mechanisms.md), [M023 protocol](m023_protocol.md), [native eligibility audit](../datasets/m023_native_eligibility.md), and the exact revision used for the interview. Cite only executed outcomes from reports; neither a proposed experiment nor a passing unit test establishes a neuroscience finding.

## Twenty questions and answer criteria

| Question | A defensible answer should include | Inspectable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. What does an extracellular threshold crossing measure? | A voltage threshold event influenced by neurons, noise, geometry and processing; distinguish multiunit crossings from isolated cells and from firing-rate estimates. Explain LINK's count description versus NWB volts-unit conflict. | [LINK loader](../../src/cerebro/link.py), [SENSE notes](../learning/sense_01.md) |
| 2. How do electrode, channel and unit differ? | Physical contact, acquisition signal path and inferred sorted neuronal source; matching contact coordinates does not prove stable neurons across dates. | [Contact mapping](../../src/cerebro/link.py), [native audit](../datasets/m023_native_eligibility.json) |
| 3. What is a time-by-unit matrix, and how does bin width change it? | Rows are observation intervals, columns defined neural features; counts are dimensionless events, rates require exposure duration. Discuss sparsity, temporal resolution and excluded trial tails. | [Count characterization](../../src/cerebro/characterize.py), [tests](../../tests/test_characterize.py) |
| 4. How would you certify observed silence? | Require documented observation coverage, valid timestamps and quality exclusions; first/last spike extrema cannot establish acquisition coverage. Never fill unknown gaps with zeros. | [Native coverage audit](../datasets/m023_native_eligibility.md), [LINK tests](../../tests/test_link.py) |
| 5. What is the difference between PCA and factor analysis? | PCA maximizes retained variance with orthogonal axes; FA specifies latent shared covariance plus diagonal observation noise. Neither implies causal biological factors. Compare held-out evidence under compatible preprocessing. | [Population models](../../src/cerebro/population.py), [tests](../../tests/test_population.py) |
| 6. Why can two equivalent latent models have different coordinates? | Rotations can preserve reconstructions and subspaces; transform transition and observation operators consistently. Compare principal angles or invariant predictions rather than coordinate-wise correlations alone. | [Population geometry](../../src/cerebro/population.py), [rotation check](../../src/cerebro/research_dynamics.py) |
| 7. Write a linear latent dynamical model and its assumptions. | State transition, observation map, process/measurement noise, intercepts and initial state; identify Gaussian/linearity assumptions, nonstationarity and count-data mismatch. Separate a latent AR fit from a fully estimated probabilistic state-space model. | [Latent AR analysis](../../src/cerebro/research_dynamics.py), [Kalman implementation](../../src/cerebro/kalman.py) |
| 8. How do filtering and smoothing differ? | Filtering conditions on observations available up to the prediction time; smoothing uses future observations. Explicitly audit same-bin features, bin completion and latency before claiming causality. | [Kalman prediction](../../src/cerebro/kalman.py), [sequence benchmark](../../src/cerebro/sequence_benchmark.py) |
| 9. What makes a multi-step neural forecast meaningful? | Trial-local transitions, no teacher-forced future states during recursive rollout, stable evaluation scale, horizon-specific uncertainty, persistence/mean/permutation controls. | [M023 dynamics](../../src/cerebro/research_dynamics.py), [tests](../../tests/test_research_dynamics.py) |
| 10. Can a stable-looking manifold establish stable computation? | No: task mix, firing statistics, recording quality and projection choice can explain visual stability. Require condition controls and held-out predictions; physiology remains unidentified. | [M023 protocol](m023_protocol.md), [geometry helpers](../../src/cerebro/population.py) |
| 11. Derive ridge regression and explain the scaling dependence. | Minimize squared error plus coefficient penalty; discuss intercept, singular values, effective degrees of freedom and why rescaling changes the prior. Use linear solves rather than explicit matrix inversion. | [Decoder fitting](../../src/cerebro/population.py), [M022 report](../../reports/m022_calibration_mechanisms.md) |
| 12. Why might fifteen trials fail despite thousands of bins? | Within-trial dependence, limited command coverage, effective sample size and feature dimension; distinguish estimator variability from physiological drift. | [M022 experiments](../../src/cerebro/calibration_mechanisms.py), [report](../../reports/m022_calibration_mechanisms.md) |
| 13. What information does balanced calibration consume? | Labels or commands for the allocation pool, later-trial access where relevant, selected behavioral labels, and unlabeled neural observations. Fairness requires an explicit access budget. | [M022 protocol](m022_protocol.md), [allocation tests](../../tests/test_calibration_mechanisms.py) |
| 14. Why could shrinkage help and still fail the study? | Convex prediction mixing reduces recalibration variance while retaining source bias; a plausible mechanism is distinct from satisfying prespecified practical-effect and every-date gates. | [M022 outcomes](../../reports/m022_calibration_mechanisms.md) |
| 15. How can reliability be predicted without evaluation-label leakage? | Restrict predictors to permitted calibration information; keep outcome labels confined to development; fit scalers and risk models inside session-held-out folds; freeze parameters before new-session scoring. | [Reliability model](../../src/cerebro/reliability.py), [tests](../../tests/test_reliability.py), [M023 protocol](m023_protocol.md) |
| 16. Which label-only controls challenge a neural-decoder claim? | Calibration target mean and other permitted label-only features; distinguish intercept correction, which retains frozen neural predictions. Improvement may reflect target offsets rather than recovered neural information. | [M022 controls](../../src/cerebro/calibration_mechanisms.py), [M023 reliability](../../src/cerebro/reliability.py) |
| 17. Where can preprocessing leak across trials or sessions? | Global scaling, feature selection, hyperparameters, smoothed labels, sequence windows, condition pooling and repeated test inspection; partition before fitting and never bridge trial boundaries. | [Longitudinal implementation](../../src/cerebro/longitudinal.py), [sequence tests](../../tests/test_sequence_benchmark.py) |
| 18. What is the independent unit for uncertainty? | Trial blocks for conditional within-session uncertainty; sessions for session generalization; animals for biological generalization. Calibration resamples are not new animals or dates. | [Block intervals](../../src/cerebro/longitudinal.py), [M022 limitations](../../reports/m022_calibration_mechanisms.md) |
| 19. How do signal and residual correlations differ? | Condition-dependent expected responses versus deviations conditioned on event time and condition; replication and effective rank limit residual covariance. Neither correlation identifies connectivity. | [Event analysis](../../src/cerebro/event_aligned.py), [learning notes](../learning/event_04.md), [Brochier report](../../reports/mission_020_brochier_population.md) |
| 20. What would falsify a claimed adaptation advance? | Prespecified baselines, information budgets, practical effect and uncertainty fail on untouched eligible sessions. Preserve all outcomes, distinguish software correctness from hypothesis success, and do not recycle tests. | [M022 negative result](../../reports/m022_calibration_mechanisms.md), [M023 protocol](m023_protocol.md) |

## Live exercises

1. **Trial-safe decoder evaluation, 20 minutes.** Given variable-length trials containing counts, targets, timestamps and identities, implement a training-only scaler, ridge decoder and equal-trial MSE. Demonstrate chronological disjoint splits, rejected missing coverage, no cross-trial windows and a label-mean baseline. Explain how equal-bin and equal-trial estimands differ. Review against [longitudinal tests](../../tests/test_longitudinal.py).
2. **Latent invariance and forecasting, 15 minutes.** Write the coordinate change for an orthogonal rotation of a latent linear system; show unchanged observation forecasts. Construct one-step and recursive five-step predictions without crossing trials. Explain why reconstruction, forecast and predictive likelihood are different metrics. Review against [dynamics tests](../../tests/test_research_dynamics.py).
3. **Prospective adaptation decision, 20 minutes.** Design a calibration-only harm diagnostic and a session-held-out development gate. List every label, condition and neural sample accessed. Specify null/label-only controls, a practical threshold and a stopping rule before revealing outcomes. Explain why three sessions from one animal cannot establish population-wide reliability. Review against [reliability tests](../../tests/test_reliability.py).

Answers should make assumptions, dimensions, physical units, information access and failure modes explicit. These exercises assess understanding; their presence in this pack does not certify that the researcher has completed them.
