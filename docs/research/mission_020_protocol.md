# Mission 020 frozen Brochier population protocol

Frozen before computing Mission 020 neural population outcomes. Configuration: [brochier_population_20.json](../../configs/brochier_population_20.json). The [feasibility audit](mission_020_feasibility.md) establishes trial counts, event meaning, native sampled coverage, and covariance rank limits. This is an observational, within-recording study; no decoder or model-family selection occurs. The human researcher retains ownership of interpretation.

**Question, prediction and failure criterion.** Describe whether source-designated single-unit populations change their count activity around recorded table-switch release and exhibit trial-to-trial shared fluctuations after condition/time expectations are removed. A paired post-minus-pre population rate near zero would not support an event-related mean change. A residual population-mean variance ratio near its independent-unit reference of one would not support detectable aggregate shared fluctuations. Neither outcome diagnoses a causal circuit mechanism. All results, including null/negative estimates, are retained.

**Eligibility and clocks.** Analyze L and N separately, never assume corresponding neurons. Use the Mission 019 checksum-pinned sorted NEV, odML and sampled raw NS5/NS6. Include only `PerformanceCode=255` trials in the four recorded SGLF/SGHF/PGLF/PGHF classes with an `SR` tick matching NEV and full sampled neural coverage inside source trial boundaries. Align `[SR−200 ms, SR+400 ms)` in 30 half-open 20-ms bins (600 ticks each at 30 kHz); no smoothing, interpolation or extrapolation. Preserve source trial IDs, event ticks and sorted SUA IDs; exclude noise and MUA. Stop if any class has fewer than 20 eligible trials. Expected tensor shapes from the pre-outcome audit are L `135×30×93` and N `142×30×156`.

**Prespecified measurements.** Convert counts to Hz by dividing each 20-ms count by 0.02 s. Report condition-specific mean unit and population-per-unit rate trajectories, plus the paired whole-trial post `[0,+400)` minus pre `[−200,0)` mean-unit rate. For each condition and unit, compute the across-trial Fano factor of total 400-ms post-event spike counts using sample variance (`ddof=1`) divided by mean; main summaries include only cells with mean count at least one and report exclusions. Define *signal-like* unit correlation across the 4×30 condition/time expected rate vectors; condition and time are not independent samples. For residuals, subtract the matching condition-and-time mean from every trial/bin/unit count, then average the twenty post-event residual rate bins within each trial. Estimate trial-level pairwise residual correlation from these whole-trial vectors after condition demeaning; report the distribution and finite/undefined pair counts, never a reliable full matrix or per-pair inference. The maximum single-stratum residual covariance rank is reported. A shared-fluctuation ratio is the variance of the residual population-mean rate divided by the sum of unit residual variances scaled by `1/N²`; one is the diagonal-only reference, with zero/undefined denominators reported. For population dimensionality, calculate the participation ratio of the centered 4×30 condition-mean rate matrix and the first two PCA coordinates for descriptive condition and fixed first-three-trial trajectories. This is not latent/intrinsic neural dimension or held-out dynamics.

**Uncertainty and controls.** Use 300 fixed-seed whole-trial bootstrap draws stratified within condition for scalar paired rate change, median eligible Fano, median absolute residual correlation, shared-fluctuation ratio and signal participation ratio. Undefined draws remain explicitly counted; report percentile bands only when enough finite draws exist. Use 200 within-condition, independently per-unit trial permutations as a reference for residual median absolute correlation and shared-fluctuation ratio. This destroys simultaneous trial fluctuations while preserving each unit's condition distribution; it does not establish independence or rule out temporal drift. Show L and N separately. Report early/late trial-half sensitivity for aggregate residual sharing. All intervals are conditional on trials in one recorded session, not confidence intervals over animals. No multiple-comparison or pairwise significance claims.

**Output and reproducibility.** Write a machine-readable report, one concise scientific report, and labeled figures showing source, animal, event, physical units, window, and statistical limits. Record source hashes, config hash, code hash, Python/library versions, exact trial IDs, tensor shapes, exclusion reasons and numerical checks: native coverage, event match, spike conservation, nonnegative integer counts, residual condition/time sums near zero, and deterministic rerun hash. Keep raw recordings ignored. Historical Mission 019 results are immutable.
