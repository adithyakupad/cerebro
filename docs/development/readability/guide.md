# Personal code-comprehension guide

Trace these ten functions in order. For each, write down the input shapes/units, the state before the first calculation, which observations are permitted, the transformation sequence and the output contract. Then reconstruct a small version from those notes without copying the source; use the existing tests to challenge your version. Synthetic exercises explain software, not neuroscience findings.

1. **[`sense.bin_spikes`](../../../src/cerebro/sense.py)** — Ordered spike seconds and observed trial intervals become time-by-unit integer counts plus coverage masks. Trace duration → complete-bin count → edge offsets → search indices → count differences → coverage. Explain why the right endpoint is excluded, why a missing cell is -1, and why a spike outside verified coverage cannot establish observed silence. Reconstruct two trials with a gap and a spike exactly on an edge. Tests: `test_sense.py`.
2. **[`sense.align_behavior`](../../../src/cerebro/sense.py)** — Native behavioral samples become bin means and a valid-bin mask. Trace units/conversion, sample selection, endpoint/internal-gap rejection, then averaging. Explain why extrapolation and silently bridging missing samples are forbidden. Reconstruct an empty bin and an internal gap. Tests: `test_sense.py`.
3. **[`benchmark.prepare`](../../../src/cerebro/benchmark.py)** — Recorded trials and a fixed event/window protocol become aligned neural/velocity tensors and original IDs. Trace training/validation labels, held-in unit selection, complete coverage and stacking. Explain when each observation becomes available. Reconstruct a small trial exclusion while retaining identity. Tests: `test_benchmark.py`.
4. **[`benchmark.run`](../../../src/cerebro/benchmark.py)** — Fixed tensors become inner-selection scores, final source-training models, validation predictions and restorable parameters. Follow masks → flattened counts/targets → training-fitted scaling → dimension/penalty selection → refit → prediction → equal-trial metrics. Explain why fitting a scaler before partitioning leaks information. Reconstruct ridge selection with one inner split. Tests: `test_benchmark.py`.
5. **[`kalman.predict_kalman`](../../../src/cerebro/kalman.py)** — Trial sequences become recursive behavioral estimates. Trace prior reset at each trial, state prediction, innovation, gain and Joseph covariance update. Explain covariance shapes, numerical solves and why contemporaneous observations do not constitute a future-independent prediction. Reconstruct one update on paper. Tests: `test_kalman.py`.
6. **[`CausalTransformer.forward`](../../../src/cerebro/sequence_models.py)** — A trials×bins×units floating tensor becomes aligned coordinates. Follow projection, sinusoidal positions, strictly future-masked attention and readout. Explain why a future input perturbation cannot change earlier outputs, and distinguish attention from physiological connectivity. Reconstruct a three-bin mask. Tests: `test_sequence_models.py`.
7. **[`self_supervised_loss`](../../../src/cerebro/representation_benchmark.py)** — Reconstructed and next-bin predictions become two explicit training objectives. Trace masked reconstruction and within-trial next-step slices separately. Explain what is learned without behavioral labels and why this does not establish useful transfer. Reconstruct a masked two-bin example and empty-mask failure. This entire historical module is hash-bound and intentionally unchanged. Tests: `test_representation_benchmark.py`.
8. **[`longitudinal.run`](../../../src/cerebro/longitudinal.py)** — Source training, target calibration and target evaluation remain separate while frozen, direct, residual and alignment controls are compared. Follow contact joins, source-only selection, each information budget and coefficient restoration. Explain why electrode correspondence is not neuron identity and why more calibration labels change the comparison. Reconstruct frozen prediction versus intercept-only correction. Tests: `test_longitudinal.py`.
9. **[`reliability.evaluate_risk`](../../../src/cerebro/reliability.py)** — Calibration-only feature rows and development outcomes become whole-session-held-out risk predictions, controls and a fixed promotion decision. Trace training-session masks, fit-only scaling, risk units (flexion⁴ MSE), shared trial resampling and stopping rules. Explain overlapping subsets versus independent sessions. Identify the separately documented nondefault-alpha permutation defect without silently correcting the historical experiment. Tests: `test_reliability.py`.
10. **[`research_dynamics.evaluate`](../../../src/cerebro/research_dynamics.py)** — A fitted static population model plus latent transition becomes held-out neural forecast/reconstruction/behavior summaries. Trace current observation → latent state → one/five transition steps → common-frame reconstruction → within-trial losses. Follow its calls to `fitted_source`, `iterate_transition` and `restore_forecast`. Explain why latent rotation, mean/persistence/permutation controls and dimensionality matching matter. Reconstruct one affine AR step and its rotated equivalent. Tests: `test_research_dynamics.py`.

## Explain the contribution independently

Use the preserved reports to answer: which experiment actually ran; what was held out; how many animals/sessions exist; what information each baseline receives; which comparison failed; and what observation could falsify your interpretation. Describe calibration variance as a statistical contributor, not an identified physiological cause. Do not call unscored later dates successful independent validation.

## Reading order after the ten functions

Follow a public CLI from `main` into `run`, read its configuration and manifest before model code, and locate its nearest failure-path test. For plotting tools, start from hash verification and then follow the data arrays to axes/units. Test fixtures deliberately use small arrays; use their controlled perturbations to explain causal timing and split safety rather than memorize numerical results.

The branch contains small explicit transformations rather than a rewritten architecture. Public schemas, numerical operation grouping, vectorized kernels and historical source guards remain intact. For unresolved correctness issues and source-hash restrictions, read [the separate proposals](correctness_proposals.md).
