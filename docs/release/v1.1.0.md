Prepared research-release notes. Publication requires explicit owner approval.

This release integrates Missions022–023 with the preserved v1.0 computational neuroengineering platform: real-data acquisition, population analysis, decoding, representation experiments, longitudinal analysis and visualization. ## Research evidence

- One LINK macaque; Jan27 source and Jan30/Feb4/Feb5 development dates. Whole-trial calibration experiments expose allocation sensitivity and finite-subset prediction dispersion. None of seven prespecified mitigations passes its frozen promotion gate.
- Calibration-only reliability features are tested with whole-session development folds and label-only, constant and shift controls. Combined risk MSE is1.093746724×10⁻⁵ flexion⁴ versus7.202293230×10⁻⁶ for label-only moments. No reliability candidate passes.
- Source PCA6 with affine latent autoregression improves one-step neural MSE42.364% versus persistence, but3.594% versus training mean and2.398% versus temporal permutation. The practical5% primary gate fails. This is a static observation model plus AR, not a jointly fitted dynamical system.
- Later Feb24/Feb25/Jun26 recordings pass native eligibility but remain unscored under stopping rules. No untouched-session adaptation benefit is claimed.

## Scientific and data limitations

These are inspected development findings from one subject. Overlapping subsets and trial bootstraps are not independent animals; intervals are conditional on fixed sessions/models. Whole-session folds are nonchronological. Contact correspondence does not identify neurons. Statistical contributors are not physiological causes. Normalized flexion is not physical displacement. FA scoring warnings and likelihood assumptions remain documented. No reliable adaptive superiority, subject invariance, foundation-model generalization, clinical application or live biological closed-loop operation is established.

Original v1.0 negative findings, FALCON upstream EMG-processing limitations, unresolved Perich neural coverage and Brochier raw-data licensing conflict remain preserved.
