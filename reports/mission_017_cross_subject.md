# Mission 017: strict cross-subject evidence status

No new cross-subject model was run. The [ranked eligibility screen](../docs/datasets/mission_017_eligibility.md) found **no presently verified corpus** meeting the simultaneous requirements for authentic neural coverage, compatible original targets, multiple biological subjects, identity-preserving partitions, asset provenance and documented reuse. This is a data/provenance gate, not a missing decoder architecture or a negative cross-subject model result.

[Indy/Loco Zenodo v2](https://zenodo.org/records/3854034) has directly archived 250 Hz position with explicit units and 47 sessions across two animals. Its sorted spike-event files do not document Loco acquisition intervals. [Indy broadband supplements](https://zenodo.org/records/1432819) cover only some Indy dates in the official list. A silent Loco bin therefore cannot be certified as recorded silence; first/last detected spikes are insufficient. [Brochier et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5892370/) publish raw M1 recordings and sensor/event data for two animals doing the same reach-to-grasp task, but exact source files, reuse terms and native timing have not been directly validated for CEREBRO. One session per animal also conflates session with subject. FALCON has explicit neural intervals but irreversible upstream EMG processing; Perich remains audit-only without authentic neural observation intervals.

Accordingly there is **no frozen Mission 017 cross-subject protocol**, zero-shot score, calibration curve or representation-transfer claim. Prior FALCON [author-preprocessed exploratory results](mission_014.md) remain unchanged. No dataset was selected by model performance, and independently sorted units were not pooled by column position.

The next evidence step is a narrow native audit of Brochier asset IDs, checksums, data terms, recorded timebase and original condition labels, or an authoritative Loco acquisition-coverage source. If a candidate clears all gates, freeze subject/session partitions, a feature-correspondence policy, prediction target, calibration budgets, controls, estimand and uncertainty rules in a committed protocol **before** inspecting held-out performance. Two animals can establish a limited two-direction transfer case study, not biological-population generality.
