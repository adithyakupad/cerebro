# Mission 018: population-variability feasibility

The [primary Brochier paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5892370/) reports four correct-trial condition counts: L 41/30/31/33 and N 35/35/35/36 (SG-LF/SG-HF/PG-LF/PG-HF). These are source-reported trial totals, **not counts validated from downloaded assets**. Unlike MC_Maze Small's 2–3 compatible repetitions per condition, they make a future condition-matched descriptive analysis plausible.

No trial-residual covariance, Fano factor, event-aligned tensor, uncertainty interval or figure was computed. Native neural coverage, event fidelity, within-condition timing, unit presence and trial exclusions have not been measured. Temporal covariance pooled over task phases would mix condition signal with residual variability and cannot substitute for condition- and event-time-specific residual covariance. A later analysis should preserve subject/session strata, use native observed-sample masks, estimate condition/time means only from admissible trials, report per-condition effective counts, and treat trials—not time bins—as uncertainty units. Cross-animal population inference remains limited to two confounded sessions.
